## Short biography

Katherine Bowen-Lewis was born in Dundee and grew up in Fife. After school she studied biology and education at the University of Glasgow and then completed a doctorate on teaching scientific concepts in secondary schools. Her thesis was supervised by Professor Henry Summers, who has remained her mentor to this day.

For ten years, Dr. Bowen-Lewis has worked as a teacher and head of department. She develops open educational resources and runs training courses on the energy transition, climate education and digital media. In 2022 she received the teaching award of the Wellcome Trust, presented by the then minister Ms Elizabeth Rothwell.
