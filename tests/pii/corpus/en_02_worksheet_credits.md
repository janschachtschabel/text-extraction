# Worksheet: Photosynthesis and the Energy Transition

**Subject:** Biology and Citizenship, Year 10
**Duration:** two lessons

## Tasks

1. Read the text about photosynthesis and underline all technical terms such as chlorophyll, glucose and carbon dioxide.
2. Explain in your own words why plants matter for the climate and how climate change affects farming in Yorkshire.
3. Discuss in your group what role the energy transition plays for the future of power supply in Britain. Look up figures from the National Grid and the Environment Agency.
4. Compare the statements of Mr Winters in the interview with the figures published by the Royal Society.
