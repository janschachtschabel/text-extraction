# Welcome evening for new families

The school welcomes every family that joined us this year. The welcome evening is a relaxed way to meet the teachers, see the classrooms and ask the questions that come up in the first weeks. Children are welcome too, and there will be tea, coffee and something to eat.

## Programme

The evening starts at 6 pm in the school hall. Our head teacher, Mr Daniel Ashworth, will welcome the families and give a short tour of the building. After the tour, the class teachers will be in their classrooms until 8 pm. The interpreters Ms Olena Kovalenko and Mr Петров will help with questions in Ukrainian and Russian.

## Contact

Please let the school office know if you are coming, so that we can plan the seating: welcome@example.org or 0113 496 0412.
