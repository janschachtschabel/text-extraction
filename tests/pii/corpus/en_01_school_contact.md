# Contact us

Welcome to the website of Riverside Community College in Manchester. This page lists the people who can help you, so that your question reaches the right desk without delay. Office hours are Monday to Friday, 8:30 to 16:00, and during the school holidays the office is staffed only in the mornings.

## Principal's office

The principal, Dr. Priya Patel, meets parents by appointment on Tuesday afternoons. Her deputy, Mr Thompson, is responsible for timetables and cover arrangements. You can reach both through the main office on 020 7946 0123 or by email at principal@example.org.

## Main office

The office is run by Margaret O'Connor and David Clarke. They handle absence reports, admissions and visitor passes. If your child is ill, please phone the office before 9:00 or send a message to office@example.org with your child's name and form. Our fax number is +44 20 7946 0199, although we rarely use it.

The college is located at 14 Mill Lane, Manchester M1 1AA. Buses stop right outside the main gate, and the nearest tram stop is Piccadilly Gardens.

## Departments

The Science department is led by Professor Alan Whitfield, who works closely with the University of Manchester on a project about renewable energy and photosynthesis for Years 9 and 10. Questions about the project can be sent to science@example.com.

The Parent Teacher Association raises money for trips and equipment. If you would like to donate, please transfer your gift to account IBAN GB82 WEST 1234 5698 7654 32 and write "PTA donation" as the reference. Our treasurer, Mrs Hargreaves, will gladly issue a receipt. The school shop has its own phone line, which you can reach on (212) 555-0147 during lunch breaks.

## Privacy

We use your details only to answer your enquiry. More information is available at https://www.riverside-college.example.org/privacy. For questions about data protection, please contact our data protection officer, Mr Oliver Finch, at dpo@example.org.
